SKILLS · INTERESTS

Hi there! I'm Claire, and I like finding structure in complex things (or, in other words, solving problems). I'm a Mathematics and Computing student at Georgia Tech, and love experimenting at the intersection of mathematical thinking and software to bring my ideas to life. Whether I'm exploring patterns in financial markets, working with mathematical structures, or building interactive models, I enjoy taking something abstract and figuring out how to make it tangible.

Currently, I'm a Software Engineering Intern at Georgia Tech Research Institute where I work on mathematical formalization and automated reasoning. I build tools for extracting mathematical structure, representing dependencies as graphs, and translating informal mathematics into formal code.

My work has taken me across a pretty wide range of problems, from modeling dynamical systems in AR/VR to studying market behavior and building quantitative trading models. Outside of my day-to-day work, I'm usually learning something new, working on a side project, or getting involved with the quant and finance community at Georgia Tech. These experiences have shaped how I approach problems: finding the structure underneath them and turning complex ideas into something useful.

In my spare time, you can usually find me singing, designing and drawing, listening to a wide variety of music, and hiking (occasionally).
