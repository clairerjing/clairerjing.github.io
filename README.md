# clairerjing.com

Hi! I'm Claire 👋

This is my little corner of the internet: a portfolio for the things I build, study, and get curious about. I'm a Mathematics & Computing student at Georgia Tech, and most of my work lives somewhere between math, software, data, and quantitative research.

I wanted my website to feel a little more like me than a traditional portfolio, so I built it around mathematics in motion. Instead of static graphics, the site uses interactive mathematical systems: networks become constellations, percolation becomes a galaxy, and structure emerges from disorder.

## ✦ The idea

The homepage begins as a bond percolation model on a lattice. As you move through the system, connections form, clusters grow, and the network approaches its critical threshold.

Around criticality, that same structure reorganizes into a logarithmic spiral galaxy. Keep going, and it resolves into concentric rings inspired by harmony and frequency.

The important part to me was that these aren't separate animations being swapped in and out. Each state grows out of the one before it.

## ✦ What's inside

Beyond the homepage, the site is a home for my:

- research and technical projects
- software engineering work
- quantitative finance experiments
- experience and resume
- occasional overly-specific mathematical ideas I thought would look cool on a website

## ✦ Built with

The entire site is intentionally lightweight: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, SVG, and Canvas.

A lot of the fun was figuring out how to turn mathematical ideas into interactions that still feel intuitive rather than like a visualization from a textbook.
